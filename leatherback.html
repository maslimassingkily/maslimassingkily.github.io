Leatherback turtle ecology, genetics & connectivity

This project integrated ecological, genetic, regulatory, and molecular evidence to improve understanding and conservation of leatherback turtles nesting in Sumatra.

Population trends

A long-term nesting dataset was used to examine seasonal and annual patterns in nesting activity.

Population genetics

Mitochondrial control-region sequences were used to investigate connectivity among leatherback populations in the Indian Ocean and West Pacific.

eDNA monitoring

eDNA sampling was evaluated as a non-invasive approach for detecting leatherback turtle genetic material in seawater following nesting.

Conservation implications

The combined evidence supports spatially explicit management and continued investigation of connectivity between ocean basins.
