Essential Ecosystem Area modelling

Applied conservation planning to develop approaches for protecting priority reptile species occurring outside Indonesia's formal protected-area network.

Approach

Spatial and ecological information is used to identify landscapes and habitats important for priority species.

Policy

The work connects biodiversity evidence with provincial conservation planning and formal recognition mechanisms.

Species focus

Priority reptile species, including freshwater turtles, provide focal cases for developing practical conservation models.

Outcome

The objective is to translate ecological evidence into durable habitat protection beyond conventional protected areas.
