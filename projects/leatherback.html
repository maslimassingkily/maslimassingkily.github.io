Leatherback turtle genetics & migration

Sumatra · PhD research · completed 2025
Doctoral research integrating population trends, mtDNA population genetics, eDNA-based monitoring, and conservation-policy analysis to understand leatherback turtles in Sumatra.

Overview

The project examined how ecological, genetic, and regulatory evidence can be combined to improve conservation of leatherback turtles nesting in Sumatra.

Research components

Long-term nesting monitoring, mitochondrial control-region analysis, eDNA sampling, and analysis of Indonesian conservation regulations were integrated across the thesis.

Key outputs

The work produced peer-reviewed research on eDNA-based monitoring and connectivity among leatherback populations in the Indian Ocean and West Pacific.
