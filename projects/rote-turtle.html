Rote Island snake-necked turtle conservation

Conservation work focused on the Critically Endangered Rote Island snake-necked turtle (Chelodina mccordi) and its freshwater habitat on Rote Island, East Nusa Tenggara.

Species conservation

Species-priority planning and conservation actions have focused on recovery and habitat protection.

Habitat protection

Conservation planning has included priority lakes and Essential Ecosystem Area approaches.

Reintroduction

The programme has supported planning and implementation related to reintroduction of the species to Rote Island.

Partners

The work involves collaboration with conservation organisations and government stakeholders.
