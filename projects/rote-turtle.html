Rote Island snake-necked turtle reintroduction

Rote Island, East Nusa Tenggara · 2016–present
Conservation work focused on the Critically Endangered Rote Island snake-necked turtle, including habitat protection, reintroduction planning, and conservation assessment.

Overview

The project supports species-priority conservation for the Rote Island snake-necked turtle and its remaining freshwater habitat.

Habitat and reintroduction

Work includes habitat assessment, conservation planning, and support for reintroduction approaches on Rote Island.

Conservation planning

The species has also provided a focal case for Essential Ecosystem Area development and broader freshwater turtle conservation in Indonesia.
