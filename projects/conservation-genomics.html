Conservation genomics & population assessment

A research direction using targeted nuclear genomic approaches to improve resolution of population structure, connectivity, and management units for threatened turtles.

Rationale

Nuclear genomic markers can provide higher-resolution information on population structure than single mitochondrial loci alone.

Approach

Targeted SNP genotyping, including GT-seq approaches, provides a practical framework for developing species-specific genomic panels and applying them to conservation samples.

Conservation application

TThe long-term goal is to resolve population structure and connectivity and strengthen evidence-based management units across Indonesian and Indo-Pacific turtle populations.
