Essential Ecosystem Area modelling

East Nusa Tenggara · 2016–present
Applied conservation planning to develop habitat-protection models for priority reptile species occurring outside Indonesia's formal protected-area network.

Overview

The work connects biodiversity evidence, spatial analysis, and government planning to identify and formalise important habitats outside conventional protected areas.

Species and habitat

Priority reptile species and their habitats provide practical cases for translating ecological information into spatial conservation measures.

Conservation outcome

The objective is durable habitat protection through mechanisms that can complement Indonesia's protected-area system.
