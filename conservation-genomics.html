Conservation genomics & population assessment

A developing research direction focused on nuclear genomic tools for resolving population structure, connectivity, and management units in threatened turtles.

Rationale

Nuclear genomic markers can provide higher-resolution information on population structure than a single mitochondrial locus.

Targeted genotyping

Targeted SNP approaches such as GT-seq provide a practical framework for building a species-specific genomic panel and applying it to conservation samples.

Management

The goal is to improve identification of biologically meaningful populations and support evidence-based management units.

Future directions

The approach can be extended across Indonesian and Indo-Pacific turtle populations as suitable reference samples become available.
