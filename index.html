About

My work integrates field ecology, molecular ecology, population genetics, and conservation planning to support evidence-based management of threatened species and ecosystems.

I have worked with NGOs, government agencies, research institutions, and international partners on biodiversity monitoring, turtle conservation, conservation policy, species assessments, and applied research.

I completed my PhD in Marine Animal Ecology at Wageningen University & Research, where my doctoral research focused on the ecology, genetics, and conservation of leatherback turtles in Sumatra.

Based in Indonesia · Open to research, conservation, consultancy, teaching, and international collaboration.
